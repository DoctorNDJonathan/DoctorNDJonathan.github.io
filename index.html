About

Academic grounding, industry experience

I am an Assistant Professor and Area Focal Head for Analytics & IT at XIME Bangalore, and a Ph.D. scholar at Christ University. My doctoral work applies Graph Neural Networks to Scope 3 emissions mapping.

Before academia I worked across banking, technology and consulting — as a Credit Analyst at ICICI Bank, Associate Business Development Manager at Jeevan Technologies, and Project Consultant at Aparajitha Corporate Services. That mix shapes how I teach and consult: rigorous methods, applied to real business constraints.
